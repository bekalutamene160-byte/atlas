# Atlas Pump Maintenance Manual

## General Maintenance Procedures

### Daily Inspection
- Check for unusual noise or vibration
- Inspect for leaks at seal areas
- Verify pressure gauge readings are within normal range
- Check bearing temperature (should not exceed 70 C)

### Weekly Maintenance
- Check oil level in bearing housing
- Inspect coupling alignment
- Clean pump exterior
- Record operating hours

### Monthly Maintenance
- Change lubrication oil (ISO VG 46 for Atlas-3, ISO VG 68 for Atlas-5)
- Inspect mechanical seal for wear
- Check motor current draw
- Tighten all bolts to specified torque

### Annual Overhaul
- Disassemble pump completely
- Inspect impeller for cavitation damage
- Replace mechanical seal
- Inspect shaft for wear
- Rebalance impeller
- Perform hydrostatic test at 1.5x rated pressure

## Safety Procedures

### Lockout-Tagout (LOTO)
Before performing any maintenance:
1. Notify all affected personnel
2. Shut down the pump using normal stopping procedure
3. Open the main electrical disconnect
4. Apply lockout device and tag
5. Verify zero energy state
6. Release any stored energy (pressure, springs, etc.)

### Personal Protective Equipment
Required PPE for maintenance:
- Safety glasses with side shields
- Hearing protection (when pump was recently operating)
- Steel-toe boots
- Cut-resistant gloves
- Face shield (when grinding or cutting)

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Low Flow Rate
Possible causes:
- Clogged impeller
- Worn wear rings
- Air in system
- Wrong rotation direction

### Problem: High Vibration
Possible causes:
- Misalignment
- Worn bearings
- Cavitation
- Impeller imbalance

### Problem: Seal Leakage
Possible causes:
- Worn mechanical seal
- Improper installation
- High temperature
- Chemical attack
