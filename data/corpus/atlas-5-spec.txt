ATLAS-5 CENTRIFUGAL PUMP - SPECIFICATION SHEET
================================================

Model: Atlas-5
Type: Multi-stage centrifugal pump
Manufacturer: Atlas Industrial Pumps

OPERATING SPECIFICATIONS
------------------------
Maximum operating pressure: 8 MPa (8000 kPa)
Maximum operating temperature: 95 C (95 degrees Celsius)
Rated flow rate: 120 m3/h (120 cubic meters per hour)
Impeller diameter: 300 mm (300 millimeters)
Motor power: 15 kW (15 kilowatts)
Weight (without motor): 85 kg

LUBRICATION
-----------
Bearing lubricant: ISO VG 68 mineral oil
Lubrication interval: 750 operating hours

INSPECTION INTERVALS
--------------------
Seal inspection: 500 operating hours
Bearing inspection: 1500 operating hours
Impeller inspection: 2500 operating hours

SAFETY
------
Maximum noise level: 88 dB at 1 meter
PPE required: Safety glasses, hearing protection, steel-toe boots, gloves
Emergency stop: Located on motor control panel
