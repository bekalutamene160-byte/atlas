ATLAS-3 CENTRIFUGAL PUMP - SPECIFICATION SHEET
================================================

Model: Atlas-3
Type: Single-stage centrifugal pump
Manufacturer: Atlas Industrial Pumps

OPERATING SPECIFICATIONS
------------------------
Maximum operating pressure: 5 MPa (5000 kPa)
Maximum operating temperature: 80 C (80 degrees Celsius)
Rated flow rate: 90 m3/h (90 cubic meters per hour)
Impeller diameter: 250 mm (250 millimeters)
Motor power: 11 kW (11 kilowatts)
Weight (without motor): 75 kg

LUBRICATION
-----------
Bearing lubricant: ISO VG 46 mineral oil
Lubrication interval: 500 operating hours

INSPECTION INTERVALS
--------------------
Seal inspection: 1000 operating hours
Bearing inspection: 2000 operating hours
Impeller inspection: 3000 operating hours

SAFETY
------
Maximum noise level: 85 dB at 1 meter
PPE required: Safety glasses, hearing protection, steel-toe boots
Emergency stop: Located on motor control panel
