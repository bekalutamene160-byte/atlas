What is ATLAS?

🧠 Three-Component Agent

Unlike standard RAG demos that retrieve chunks and generate answers, ATLAS uses a critic component that checks every claim against retrieved evidence. If the critic finds unsupported claims, the agent loops back and re-synthesizes — preventing hallucinations before they reach the user.

📚 Industrial Maintenance Corpus

The knowledge base contains four professional references covering equipment maintenance, OSHA machine guarding, EN 13306 maintenance standards, and reliability-centered maintenance programs. Documents are chunked and embedded with Jina v3 for semantic retrieval.

Knowledge Base (4 Documents, 2,067 Chunks)
📘
V-TECS Industrial Maintenance Guide for Mechanic
592 pages · 1986 · South Carolina Dept. of Education
Comprehensive task-based guide covering belt drives, chain & sprocket drives, gear drives, flexible couplings, bearings, hydraulic systems, boilers, cranes, elevators, welding, carpentry, drill press, grinding, milling, turning, and inspection procedures.
🛡️
Machine Guarding Checklist (OSHA 3067)
2 pages · NJ State AFL-CIO · OSHA grant SH-31243-SH7
39-point OSHA compliance checklist covering safeguard requirements, point-of-operation hazards, power transmission apparatus, electric hazards, training, PPE, and lockout/tagout procedures (29 CFR 1910.147).
📋
MaintMaster Maintenance Manual 2024
87 pages · 2024 · MaintMaster Systems
European-standard (EN 13306) maintenance reference covering the maintenance pyramid, vision/strategy/policy, preventive/corrective/ improvement maintenance, KPIs (MTTR, MOTBF, MWT), planning, spare parts management, LEAN/5S, and systematic work environment.
⚙️
Types of Maintenance Programs (O&M Guide)
9 pages · Release 3.0 · U.S. DOE Federal Energy Mgmt
Comparison of four maintenance philosophies: reactive ($18/hp/yr), preventive ($13/hp/yr), predictive ($9/hp/yr), and reliability-centered ($6/hp/yr). Covers the bathtub curve, failure modes, and RCM implementation steps from NASA 2000.
